# 01 — Product vision

## One-liner

Worksyzo is a **multi-tenant B2B SaaS** that gives each organization a **private AI employee** with access to that org’s knowledge, memory, and work — never another tenant’s.

## Problem

SME owners and managers in India lose time to:

- “Where is that policy?”
- “What did we decide in last month’s meeting?”
- “Who owns this task?”
- Tribal knowledge stuck in WhatsApp, email, and people’s heads

Enterprise tools (Glean-class) are too expensive and heavy for 20–200 person companies.

## Solution

One product surface:

1. **Knowledge** — upload / index org documents; search with citations  
2. **Memory** — decisions, meetings, notes, conversations as first-class org memory  
3. **Work** — tasks, projects, assignees, deadlines  
4. **AI** — answer, search, and (with permission) create/update work items  

## Ideal customer profile (V1 sales)

**Primary:** Small/medium companies in India (≈20–200 employees)

- Owner or ops/HR lead is buyer
- Needs simple setup, clear pricing in INR
- Tolerates “upload docs + invite team” as V1 (no heavy integrations yet)

**Secondary (later):** Schools / NGOs / shops  

**Explicitly later:** Hospitals / regulated healthcare (compliance & liability)

## Differentiation

| Not this | This |
|----------|------|
| Generic PDF chatbot | Org system of record + AI |
| Glean for enterprises | Affordable, simple, customizable for Indian SMEs |
| Feature dump Day 1 | Horizontal platform; vertical sales focus |

## Non-goals (product)

- Competing on “100+ connectors” in year one  
- Being a full HRIS / ERP / LMS  
- Public consumer AI chat  

## Success metrics (early)

- Paying orgs who upload ≥20 documents and invite ≥5 users  
- Weekly active users asking AI ≥3×/week  
- Tasks created via AI or UI used as real work tracking  
- Churn driven by “didn’t use” vs “too complex” (optimize for simplicity)
