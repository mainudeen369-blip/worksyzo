Acme Manufacturing — Leave Policy (sample for Worksyzo demo)

1. Casual leave
- Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year.
- Casual leave does not carry forward to the next year.
- Requests must be submitted at least 2 working days in advance, except emergencies.

2. Earned leave
- Employees earn 1.25 days of earned leave per completed month of service.
- Up to 30 days of earned leave may be carried forward.
- Encashment of earned leave is allowed only at resignation or retirement, subject to Finance approval.

3. Approval flow
- Leave requests go first to the reporting manager.
- After manager approval, HR confirms and records the leave in the attendance system.
- Any leave longer than 5 consecutive days also needs Admin approval.

4. Sick leave
- Up to 8 sick leave days are available per year with a medical certificate for absences over 2 days.

5. Annual day and special closures
- Company-declared holidays and the Annual Day do not consume casual or earned leave.
